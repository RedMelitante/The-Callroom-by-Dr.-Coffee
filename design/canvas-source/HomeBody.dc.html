Why the 2nd floor

Named after the room where doctors rest

We opened inside Madonna Hospital for a simple reason: the people here rarely get a real break. A good cup, made quickly and made right, is the least we can do.

In a hospital, the Callroom is where staff catch a few minutes of rest between rounds. That’s exactly what we wanted the space to be. Families waiting on news, med reps and visitors are just as welcome.
